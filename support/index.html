Support

Help for the tknz Stocks iOS app (com.tknz.stocks), developed by MOBI MEDIA, INC.

Contact us

A dedicated support email is not yet published. The current way to reach us is to open an issue on this site’s public GitHub repository:

github.com/retroservices1121/tknz-stocks-site/issues

GitHub issues are public. Do not post passwords, one-time codes, private keys, recovery phrases, or other secrets.

We also post product notes on X at @tknzstocks. X is not a substitute for a support request.

Frequently asked questions

Wallet and sign-in

How do I sign in?

tknz Stocks uses Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP) for sign-in. You can sign in with an email one-time code, an SMS one-time code, Google, or Apple. Signing in creates a Base EVM smart account for you.

How do I sign out?

Open the Account screen and tap Sign out.

Trades

What can I trade?

You can swap Base USDC for a Coinbase tokenized stock, and a tokenized stock back to USDC, on Base. Trades are executed through Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP) Trade.

Trading is only available when the eligibility gate clears (see Eligibility). Otherwise the catalog is read-only.

Is the NAV shown the price I’ll get?

No. nav_price is a Chainlink total-return reference, not a live execution or swap price. Feeds can go stale outside market hours. One B20 token is not permanently one share; the share-equivalent count uses the current multiplier.

Fees

What does tknz charge?

A 0.15% tknz fee is collected onchain on each trade. It is taken from the amount you pay, before the swap: an ERC-20 transfer of the fee in the token you pay, after which the swap uses only the remainder. The fee is included in the amount you pay, not added on top.

Failed or unfinished trades

What happens if a trade doesn’t complete?

An open swap attempt is kept visible in the app across restarts, so you can see it and follow up if a trade does not complete.

If you need help with an unfinished trade, open a GitHub issue. You can include your smart account address and any transaction hash shown in the app (these are already public onchain). Never share one-time codes or secrets.

Eligibility and geo-restriction

Who can trade?

Trading is offered under Regulation S to people outside the United States. U.S. persons and people in restricted jurisdictions cannot open a buy path.

  • App Store storefronts exclude the United States and U.S. territories.
  • The in-app gate also blocks the United States, U.S. territories, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and Syria.
  • If your country cannot be determined, the gate does not fail open.
How does the app know my country?

The app derives a coarse country from your IP address using Cloudflare, falling back to GeoJS. It does not use GPS or precise location.

Account deletion

How do I delete my account?

In-app account deletion is not available yet. It is planned. Today the app offers sign-out only.

To request deletion now, open a GitHub issue titled “Account deletion request” and include the CDP sign-in identifier you used (email or phone number) and/or your smart account address. Because issues are public, you may prefer to include only your smart account address and tell us which sign-in method you used.

Onchain transactions and other public blockchain data on Base cannot be erased.
