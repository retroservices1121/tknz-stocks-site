Support

Help for the tknz stocks iOS app (com.tknz.stocks), developed by MOBI MEDIA, INC.

Contact us

Email us at support@mobimedia.co for help with the app, your account, or your data, including account deletion.

You can also open an issue on this site’s public GitHub repository: Open a GitHub issue

GitHub issues are public. Never post passwords, one-time codes, private keys, recovery phrases, or other secrets, and do not post your email address or phone number.

We also post product notes on X at @tknzstocks. X is not a substitute for a support request.

Frequently asked questions

Wallet and sign-in

How do I sign in?

tknz stocks uses Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP) for sign-in. You can sign in with an email one-time code, an SMS one-time code, Google, or Apple. Signing in creates a Base EVM smart account for you.

The smart account is self-custodial. tknz never sees or stores your private keys. tknz is not a custodial wallet service and cannot sign for your account or move your funds.

How do I sign out?

Open the Account screen and tap Sign out.

Trades

What can I trade?

You can swap Base USDC for a Coinbase tokenized stock, and a tokenized stock back to USDC, on Base. Trades are executed through Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP) Trade.

Trading is only available when the eligibility gate clears (see Eligibility). Otherwise the catalog is read-only.

Is the NAV shown the price I’ll get?

No. nav_price is a Chainlink total-return reference, not a live execution or swap price. Feeds can go stale outside market hours. One B20 token is not permanently one share; the share-equivalent count uses the current multiplier.

Fees

What does tknz charge?

A 0.15% tknz fee is collected onchain on each trade. It is taken from the amount you pay, before the swap: an ERC-20 transfer of the fee in the token you pay, after which the swap uses only the remainder. The fee is included in the amount you pay, not added on top.

Failed or unfinished trades

What happens if a trade doesn’t complete?

An open swap attempt is kept visible in the app across restarts, so you can see it and follow up if a trade does not complete.

If you need help with an unfinished trade, email support@mobimedia.co or open a GitHub issue. You can include your smart account address and any transaction hash shown in the app (these are already public onchain). Never share one-time codes or secrets.

Eligibility and geo-restriction

Who can trade?

Trading is offered under Regulation S to people outside the United States. U.S. persons and people in restricted jurisdictions cannot open a buy path.

  • App Store storefronts exclude the United States and U.S. territories.
  • The in-app gate also blocks the United States, U.S. territories, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and Syria.
  • If your country cannot be determined, the gate does not fail open.
How does the app know my country?

The app derives a coarse country from your IP address using Cloudflare, falling back to GeoJS. It does not use GPS or precise location.

Account deletion

How do I delete my account?

Open the Account screen, choose Delete account, and confirm by typing DELETE. The app then sends the deletion request to our server with a POST to https://api.tknzstocks.com/v1/account-deletion.

tknz revokes any delegated access it has to your wallet, deletes the tknz account data it holds for your account, and records a request for tknz to have Coinbase remove your Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP) sign-in record. That removal is processed by tknz with Coinbase and is not instant. Until Coinbase removes the CDP sign-in record, signing in again with the same method may reopen the same wallet. After a successful deletion, tknz data is removed from your device and you are signed out.

Deletion does not move or delete your USDC or stock tokens. They stay in your self-custodial smart account on Base. If you do not want tokens left there, move them to another wallet you control before deleting your account.

tknz never sees or stores your private keys. tknz is not a custodial wallet service and cannot sign for your account or move your funds.

Past Base transactions are public and cannot be erased. Onchain transactions and other public blockchain data cannot be deleted.

You can also email support@mobimedia.co about your account or data, including deletion.
