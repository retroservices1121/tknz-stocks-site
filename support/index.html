Frequently asked questions

Wallet and sign-in

How do I sign in?

tknz stocks uses Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP) for sign-in. You can sign in with an email one-time code, an SMS one-time code, Google, or Apple. Signing in creates a Base EVM smart account for you.

The smart account is self-custodial. The tknz app does not collect or store your wallet’s private keys. When you place a trade, the app can submit transactions through your signed-in wallet. If you gave tknz permission through Coinbase to act on your wallet’s behalf, tknz removes that access when it processes your deletion request.

How do I sign out?

Open the Account screen and tap Sign out.

Trades

What can I trade?

You can swap Base USDC for a Coinbase tokenized stock, and a tokenized stock back to USDC, on Base. Trades are executed through Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP) Trade.

Trading is only available when the eligibility gate clears (see Eligibility). Otherwise the catalog is read-only.

Is the NAV shown the price I’ll get?

No. nav_price is a Chainlink total-return reference, not a live execution or swap price. Feeds can go stale outside market hours. One B20 token is not permanently one share; the share-equivalent count uses the current multiplier.

Fees

What does tknz charge?

A 0.15% tknz fee is collected onchain on each trade. It is taken from the amount you pay, before the swap: an ERC-20 transfer of the fee in the token you pay, after which the swap uses only the remainder. The fee is included in the amount you pay, not added on top.

Failed or unfinished trades

What happens if a trade doesn’t complete?

An open swap attempt is kept visible in the app across restarts, so you can see it and follow up if a trade does not complete.

If you need help with an unfinished trade, email support@mobimedia.co or open a GitHub issue. You can include your smart account address and any transaction hash shown in the app (these are already public onchain). Never share one-time codes or secrets.

Eligibility and geo-restriction

Who can trade?

Trading is offered under Regulation S to people outside the United States. U.S. persons and people in restricted jurisdictions cannot open a buy path.

  • App Store storefronts exclude the United States and U.S. territories.
  • The in-app gate also blocks the United States, U.S. territories, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and Syria.
  • If your country cannot be determined, the gate does not fail open.
How does the app know my country?

The app derives a coarse country from your IP address using Cloudflare, falling back to GeoJS. It does not use GPS or precise location.

Account deletion

How do I delete my account?

While signed in, open the Account screen and tap Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete account again.

tknz deletes the account data it stores on its server and removes any access you gave tknz through Coinbase to act on your wallet’s behalf. tknz keeps a record of your request so its team can ask Coinbase to remove your sign-in record. Until Coinbase removes it, signing in again with the same method may reopen the same wallet. Once the tknz server accepts your request, the app tries to clear tknz data from this device and sign you out. The app tells you if either step can’t be completed.

If the app is loading saved trade details or submitting a trade, wait for that step to finish, then try again. If the app can’t read the saved trade details, it pauses deletion to protect them. If the app shows a saved trade attempt, copy the wallet address and any trade references shown, then confirm you saved the details. If the app can’t confirm that the tknz server accepted your request, the app leaves your sign-in and device data in place. The server may already have processed some or all of the request, and it’s safe to try again.

Deleting your account does not move or delete your USDC or stocks. They stay in your wallet, which you control. If you don’t want them left there, move them to another wallet you control before you delete your account.

The tknz app does not collect or store your wallet’s private keys. When you place a trade, the app can submit transactions through your signed-in wallet. If you gave tknz permission through Coinbase to act on your wallet’s behalf, tknz removes that access when it processes your deletion request.

Your past transactions are recorded on a public blockchain and cannot be erased.

You can also email support@mobimedia.co about your account or data, including deletion.
