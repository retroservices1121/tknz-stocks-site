About the app

tknz Stocks lets you browse and hold Coinbase tokenized stocks (B20) on Base. You can view the public catalog without signing in. To trade, you sign in through Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP), which creates a Base smart account, and you can swap Base USDC for a tokenized stock when the eligibility gate clears.

Data we collect and why

All data listed below is used only for app functionality. We do not use any of it to track you.

About Device ID and product interaction: third-party SDKs used by the app (CDP and Expo) can collect some data at the native level. We will re-check the Device ID and product-interaction entries against the privacy reports for those SDKs once the first iOS build is produced, and update this policy if they show anything different.

About eligibility: to decide whether you can open a buy path, the app looks up the country for your IP address (using Cloudflare, falling back to GeoJS) and may also read the region setting on your device. This is used only for the eligibility gate.

Data we do not collect

The app does not collect precise location, health data, contacts, photos, browsing history, advertising data, or other sensitive information.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

  • We do not use your data to track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising.
  • The app does not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt or the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not run first-party analytics.
  • The app does not include AdMob, Firebase, Sentry, Segment, Amplitude, AppsFlyer, Branch, or CloudKit, and it has no in-app purchases.

If a third-party SDK used by the app is later found to track users across apps and websites, we will update this policy.

Data stored on your device

  • In-progress swap record. While a swap is in progress, the app may store a record of it on your device so the attempt stays visible across app restarts.
  • Sign-in session. CDP session secrets are expected to be stored in your device’s secure storage by the CDP SDK.

Third-party services

The app connects to the following services. When your device contacts them, they receive standard network information such as your IP address. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

  • Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP) — sign-in (email, SMS, Google, Apple), smart account creation, and trades. See the Coinbase Privacy Policy.
  • Coinbase Tokenized Stocks API — the public catalog of tokenized stocks. See the Coinbase Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — IP-to-country lookup for the eligibility gate only. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • GeoJS — fallback IP-to-country lookup for the eligibility gate only.
  • Base RPC (mainnet.base.org) — reading balances and other data from the Base blockchain.
  • Basescan — a block explorer. The app may link you out to Basescan to view a contract or transaction; that site is governed by its own policies.

Google or Apple may also process data when you choose to sign in with them, under their own policies.

Public blockchain data

Trades settle onchain on Base. Your smart account address, balances, and transactions (including the 0.15% tknz fee transfer) are recorded on a public blockchain and are visible to anyone. We cannot change or delete blockchain records.

Retention

Sign-in data (such as your email address or phone number) and account data are held by Coinbase Developer Platform. We do not set CDP’s retention periods; please consult Coinbase’s privacy policy for how long CDP keeps this data.

Signing out ends your session in the app. We have not independently verified how third-party SDKs used by the app retain data on your device after the app is uninstalled. Neither signing out nor uninstalling deletes your CDP account — see Account deletion.

Account deletion

In-app account deletion is not available yet. It is planned. Today the app offers sign-out only, from the Account screen.

If you have questions about your account or data, including deletion, you can contact us by opening a GitHub issue. GitHub issues are public: never post one-time codes or other secrets, and do not post your email address or phone number there.

Onchain transactions and other public blockchain data cannot be deleted.

Children

tknz Stocks is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.

Contact

A dedicated privacy or support email is not yet published. To contact us about this policy or your data, open a GitHub issue. Issues are public: never post secrets, and do not post your email address or phone number.
